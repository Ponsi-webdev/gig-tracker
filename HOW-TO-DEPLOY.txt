GIG TRACKER - DEPLOY IN 3 MINUTES (FREE)

Option A - Netlify (easiest, no account setup tools)
1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop and sign in (free).
2. Drag the whole "gig-tracker-app" FOLDER (unzipped) onto the page.
3. Copy the link it gives you (https://something.netlify.app).

Option B - Vercel
1. Put these files in a GitHub repo, import it on vercel.com, click Deploy.

INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE
- Android (Chrome): open your link, tap the 3-dot menu, then "Install app" / "Add to Home screen".
- iPhone (Safari): open your link, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

KEEP YOUR OLD LEADS
In the old version: Stats > Copy backup. In the new app: Stats > Restore backup > paste.

ANDROID APK (optional, later)
Go to pwabuilder.com, paste your link, choose Android, download the package.
